# R3TURN

**Trade and commercial intelligence for export manufacturers.**

R3TURN is the operating and public brand of **RETURN Teknoloji ve Dış Ticaret Ltd. Şti.**, based in Istanbul, Türkiye.

We work with public, commercial and machine-readable signals to help companies understand how they are externally interpreted and where commercially relevant gaps or opportunities may exist.

## What we do

R3TURN operates through two primary intelligence products:

### Brand Intelligence

Examines how a company is read across public digital surfaces, search systems and AI systems, and identifies gaps between its real operational capability and what can be externally discovered, interpreted and verified.

### Growth Engine

Uses market, demand, trade, competitive, company and timing signals to support market prioritization and commercial opportunity analysis.

## Evidence Before Output.

Our operating principle is simple:

**Evidence Before Output.**

Public claims should be traceable to evidence, methodology or an explicit verification path.
